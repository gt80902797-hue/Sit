Refund Policy

This Refund Policy explains how refund requests for AssignMate services are handled.

Refund Requests

Refund requests may be considered when an order cannot be completed, is cancelled according to the applicable terms, or there is another valid reason determined by the platform.

Completed Orders

Once an order has been completed and accepted by the student, a refund may not be available except where required by applicable law or where the platform determines that a refund is appropriate.

Incorrect or Incomplete Work

If submitted work does not reasonably match the agreed requirements, the issue may be reviewed before deciding whether a correction, replacement, partial refund, or other resolution is appropriate.

Processing

Approved refunds will be processed using the applicable payment method or another appropriate method.

Contact

For refund-related questions, please contact us through the contact information provided on the website.
